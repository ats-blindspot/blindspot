Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. What We Collect

When you use Blindspot, we collect:

  • Device ID — a unique anonymous identifier for your device
  • Category preferences — the 3 interests you pick
  • Country — detected automatically from your IP
  • Reactions — which cards you react to or save
  • View history — which cards you've seen

2. How We Use It

We use your data only to:

  • Personalize your feed with cards you'll enjoy
  • Send you daily curiosity notifications
  • Improve the app experience

We never sell your data. Ever.

3. Third-Party Services

Blindspot uses these services to work:

  • Supabase — secure database storage
  • Groq — AI card generation
  • Tavily — fact verification
  • OneSignal — push notifications
  • Google AdMob — rewarded ads

Each has its own privacy policy.

4. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete all your data anytime via "Logout"
  • Turn off notifications in settings
  • Uninstall the app to stop all data collection

5. Contact

Questions? Email us at privacy@blindspot.app

About Blindspot
👁

What Is Blindspot?

Blindspot is a fascination engine. We expose comfortable lies we all believe — one card at a time.

Every card challenges a belief. Every layer reveals the truth. Every day is a new discovery.

Our Inspiration

We're inspired by Mel Martin — the legendary copywriter behind Boardroom's secret weapon. Mel knew that curiosity is the strongest force in human psychology.

He'd tease you with a headline. He'd make you need to know more. Then he'd deliver with facts, precision, and craft.

Blindspot is Mel Martin reimagined for 2025 — powered by AI, delivered in seconds.

How It Works

  • You pick 3 interests
  • AI generates fresh cards every day
  • Each card hides 4 layers of truth
  • Watch a short ad to unlock
  • Discover. Question. Grow.

The Rules We Follow

  • Real facts, verified
  • Mel Martin-level craft
  • Global, timeless, no names
  • The truth they hide from you
